COLA weighting testbed: two tests in the full engine

2026-07-19 · Highley & Rajan, SSAC27 working results

Carry-Over Lottery Allocation (COLA) is a National Basketball Association (NBA) draft reform in which draft priority comes from a multi-year "drought" index (roughly one unit per season out of contention) instead of the single-season record. This page reports a lottery-weighting testbed run in the full ZenGM Basketball GM engine, scored as two tests at two eligibility pools, with an interactive lottery playground at the end.

This page supersedes the earlier Pareto frontier viewer. The earlier grid's equity objective saturated, and its headline did not survive the move to the full engine; details are in the paper.

How to read this

Both tests are in pick units on the same draft board.

  • Test (a), tanking. The expected-pick gap between the worst-record team and the fifth-worst-record team, with teams ranked by the current season's record. Positive means losing pays. The target is about zero.
  • Test (b), help. Does a longer-drought team draft earlier? At the 22-team pool (E = 22) this is the same #1-vs-#5 gap with teams ranked by each mechanism's own index, and the target is a wide positive gap. At the 14-team pool (E = 14) it is the per-drought-year pick slope among pool teams, and the target is a clearly negative slope.

For scale, a first-overall pick is worth about 2-3 wins over a thirtieth pick at its peak, so help is a pick-level dial with a modest outcome channel.

Mechanisms

Classic
Top-four lottery drawn proportional to the index; picks 5-14 ordered by current record (worse record earlier).
Gamma family
Every pick drawn sequentially with probability proportional to indexgamma. Gamma = 0 is a flat lottery, gamma = 1 is proportional, and higher values are steeper.
Middle option
Top-four lottery drawn proportional to the index (same as Classic); picks 5-14 ordered by the index instead of record.

Verdict

At E = 14, the middle option clears the tanking test (its CI includes zero, against Classic's +1.94) and directs help statistically indistinguishably from Classic (−0.99 vs −0.93 picks per drought-year, Welch t about 1.5), while keeping the NBA-familiar four-pick lottery. The gamma = 1 mechanism also clears the tanking test but directs help less sharply at E = 14 (−0.57).

Lottery playground

A client-side Monte Carlo simulator over the 14-team pool. Team 1 has the worst current record and Team 14 the best record in the pool. Set each team's drought with the sliders, pick a mechanism, and the page reruns 20,000 lotteries. The default drought vector is deliberately imperfectly correlated with record so the mechanisms visibly differ.

The two summary stats mirror the tests above. The tanking gradient is the expected pick of Team 5 minus Team 1 (positive means losing pays; want about zero). The help slope is the ordinary least squares (OLS) slope of expected pick on drought-years across the 14 teams (want clearly negative).

Draw semantics
  • weighti = droughtigamma, with 00 = 1, so gamma = 0 gives every team weight 1.
  • Flat and the gamma family draw all 14 picks sequentially without replacement, each pick choosing among the remaining teams with probability proportional to weight.
  • Classic draws picks 1-4 sequentially proportional to drought (gamma = 1 weights), then assigns picks 5-14 to the remaining teams in record order, worst record first.
  • The middle option draws picks 1-4 as Classic does, then assigns picks 5-14 to the remaining teams by drought descending, ties broken by worse record first.
  • If all remaining weights are zero, the draw is uniform among the remaining teams.
